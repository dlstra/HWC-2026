Coaching Staff
The Warriors Behind the Club
Director
AJ Lyczkowski
AJ Lyczkowski is a Pennsylvania native. During his time at Methacton High School in Collegeville, PA, he compiled a 102–33 record with 65 pins and still holds the school record for the fastest pin in program history at just 5 seconds. A 3-time sectional champion, 2-time district placer, 1-time regional champion, and Pennsylvania AAA State Tournament qualifier, his success earned him a Division I scholarship to the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga, where he became a 4-year letterman with a 46–28 collegiate record and two third-place finishes at the Southern Conference Championships. Following his competitive career, he transitioned into coaching — guiding four student-athletes to regional qualification in back-to-back seasons at Olney High School. He now teaches in the Special Education Department at The Woodlands High School, where he also serves as the head wrestling coach. Read more
Head Coach
Alec Strange
Alec has been a club coach for six years and served as an assistant coach at Episcopal High School for one year. He will join The Woodlands High School coaching staff as an assistant coach this upcoming season. During his coaching career, he has helped train multiple All-Americans and contributed to the development of several athletes who earned spots on the UIL state podium.
Assistant Coach
Livan López
Livan López is a Cuban freestyle wrestler and Olympic bronze medalist, recognized internationally for his technical skill, speed, and competitive consistency at the highest levels of the sport. He earned bronze at the 2012 London Olympic Games in the 66 kg division, marking one of the highlights of his career on the world stage. López is also a 2011 World Championship bronze medalist and 2011 Pan American Games gold medalist, establishing himself as one of Cuba's most accomplished athletes of his generation. Known for his disciplined approach and ability to perform under pressure, he has represented Cuba in numerous World Championships and major international events. Today, his legacy continues to inspire wrestlers around the world through his achievements and dedication to the sport.
Assistant Coach
Charlie Strange
Charlie has been a club coach for 10 years and served as the Director of TXUSAW and a Team Texas coach for six years. Throughout his coaching career, he has helped train multiple All-Americans and contributed to the development of numerous athletes who earned spots on the UIL state podium.

Our Story

Who We Are

The Highlander Wrestling Club is a program forged to build strong athletes and even stronger character — warriors in every sense.

Purpose
Our Mission

HWC is dedicated to providing and promoting wrestling opportunities for boys and girls from elementary through high school. Our mission is to foster the physical, mental, and social development of young athletes through the sport of wrestling by offering year-round training, mentorship, and competition in a safe, positive, and supportive environment.

We are committed to helping every athlete reach their full potential both on and off the mat by emphasizing sportsmanship, discipline, teamwork, character, and personal growth while building confidence, resilience, and a lifelong love for the sport.

What We Stand For
Our Values
Discipline
We show up, work hard, and hold ourselves to a high standard every day.

The Team
Meet the Staff
Director
AJ Lyczkowski
AJ Lyczkowski is a Pennsylvania native. During his time at Methacton High School in Collegeville, PA, he compiled a 102–33 record with 65 pins and still holds the school record for the fastest pin in program history at just 5 seconds. A 3-time sectional champion, 2-time district placer, 1-time regional champion, and Pennsylvania AAA State Tournament qualifier, his success earned him a Division I scholarship to the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga, where he became a 4-year letterman with a 46–28 collegiate record and two third-place finishes at the Southern Conference Championships. Following his competitive career, he transitioned into coaching — guiding four student-athletes to regional qualification in back-to-back seasons at Olney High School. He now teaches in the Special Education Department at The Woodlands High School, where he also serves as the head wrestling coach.
Head Coach
Alec Strange
Alec has been a club coach for six years and served as an assistant coach at Episcopal High School for one year. He will join The Woodlands High School coaching staff as an assistant coach this upcoming season. During his coaching career, he has helped train multiple All-Americans and contributed to the development of several athletes who earned spots on the UIL state podium.
Assistant Coach
Charlie Strange
Charlie has been a club coach for 10 years and served as the Director of TXUSAW and a Team Texas coach for six years. Throughout his coaching career, he has helped train multiple All-Americans and contributed to the development of numerous athletes who earned spots on the UIL state podium.
Assistant Coach
Livan López
Livan López is a Cuban freestyle wrestler and Olympic bronze medalist, recognized internationally for his technical skill, speed, and competitive consistency at the highest levels of the sport. He earned bronze at the 2012 London Olympic Games in the 66 kg division, marking one of the highlights of his career on the world stage. López is also a 2011 World Championship bronze medalist and 2011 Pan American Games gold medalist, establishing himself as one of Cuba's most accomplished athletes of his generation. Known for his disciplined approach and ability to perform under pressure, he has represented Cuba in numerous World Championships and major international events. Today, his legacy continues to inspire wrestlers around the world through his achievements and dedication to the sport.
